How to combine mesh in Unity

VRToolsUnity

I used Blender to combine meshes, but sometimes I just want to combine meshes in Unity itself, instead of exporting them into Blender, combine them, them back to Unity. Therefore, I started looking for solutions, and I found this very helpful tool: Mesh Combiner 


All the detailed info is on its page, and it is very easy to use. You will see how it optimizes your FPS very quickly. And it is free. Thanks to the author of this tool, it helped me save lots of time during the VR optimization process.
